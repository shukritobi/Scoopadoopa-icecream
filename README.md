# Scoopa Doopa Ice Cream

Proposal website for **Scoopa Doopa Ice Cream**, Rawang.

## Website strategy

The site is designed around the business direction surfaced from public research and Maleek's recent social posts:

- Wedding and private-event ice cream station
- Corporate events and catering
- Recurring B2B supply for businesses using ice cream daily or weekly
- Direct WhatsApp quotation flow
- Strong founder story and social proof

## Research used

- mStar, 27 Apr 2024: founder story, homemade production, 15 flavours, popular flavours (vanilla, chocolate, strawberry), wedding/open-house orders.
- mStar, 24 Aug 2024: shift away from high-risk carnival booths toward catering and weddings.
- LinkedIn: Abdul Maleek Abdullah listed as Director at ScoopaDoopaIceCream, Rawang.
- Public business listings: Scoopa Doopa Ice Cream operates in Rawang.
- Current social screenshots supplied by the project owner: new push for entrepreneurs/businesses needing ice cream on a daily or weekly basis.
